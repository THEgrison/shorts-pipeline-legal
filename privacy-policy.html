This Privacy Policy explains how Shorts Pipeline (“Service”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our automated short-form video production and publishing tools, including integrations with TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.

1. Who this Policy applies to

This Policy applies to operators, administrators, and authorized users of Shorts Pipeline, and to individuals whose information may be processed through connected social accounts used with the Service.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you configure and use the Service, we may process:

  • Account and contact information — such as name, email address, and workspace identifiers you provide.
  • Authentication data — OAuth access/refresh tokens, account IDs, and related metadata for connected platforms (TikTok, YouTube/Google, Meta/Instagram).
  • Content and media — source videos, transcripts, clips, captions, titles, descriptions, hashtags, thumbnails, and rendered short-form videos.
  • Configuration data — niches/keywords, channel whitelists, publishing schedules, style templates, and feature flags (for example dry-run or human review).
  • Operational logs — job status, error messages, API quota counters, timestamps, and technical diagnostics needed to run and secure the Service.
  • Device/technical data — IP address, browser type, and similar technical metadata if you access a web dashboard.

We do not intentionally collect special-category sensitive personal data unless you choose to include such data in content you upload.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • operate discovery, transcription, editing, scheduling, and publishing workflows;
  • authenticate with third-party APIs and refresh tokens when needed;
  • enforce license/authorization filters and optional human review;
  • provide dashboards, alerts, metrics, and troubleshooting;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations;
  • improve reliability and performance of the Service.

AI/transcription providers may process audio/text you submit solely to generate transcripts, highlight suggestions, or platform copy, according to your configuration.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on one or more of:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you requested;
  • Legitimate interests — security, debugging, service improvement;
  • Consent — where required for specific integrations or optional processing;
  • Legal obligation — when we must retain or disclose information by law.

5. Sharing of information

We may share information with:

  • Platform providers you connect (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram) to publish content or manage your linked accounts;
  • Infrastructure/AI vendors that host compute, storage, transcription, or LLM features you enable;
  • Service operators/administrators of your deployment;
  • Authorities when legally required, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party platforms process data under their own privacy policies once content or tokens are sent to them.

6. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where cloud, AI, or social platforms operate. Where required, we take steps to protect transfers using appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including:

  • media and job artifacts while required for processing, review, or publishing;
  • account/token data while integrations remain connected;
  • logs for a limited operational period, unless a longer period is required for security or legal reasons.

Temporary working files may be deleted automatically after a configured retention window. You may request deletion of account-linked data as described below.

8. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted storage of OAuth tokens (for example Fernet encryption), access controls, and least-privilege operational practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • access, correct, or delete personal data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent where processing is consent-based;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at grison200@gmail.com. You can also disconnect third-party accounts from the relevant platform settings to revoke API access.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

If you use a web dashboard, we may use essential cookies or local storage for session/security purposes. We do not use advertising cookies in the core Service.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
